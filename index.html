05 / VOCABULARY STRATEGIES

Word Study

Words can be friends, opposites, or made from smaller parts.

✨ Explore all five mini lessons. These are examples for the homepage preview.
🤝

WORDS WITH SIMILAR MEANINGS

happy ↔ glad

These words have almost the same meaning.

I am happy.   I am glad.
↔️

WORDS WITH OPPOSITE MEANINGS

happy ↔ sad

These words have opposite meanings.

😊 Happy    😢 Sad
🧱

ADD TO THE BEGINNING

un- + happy = unhappy

The prefix un- can mean “not.”

happy → unhappy
🌱

FIND THE BASE WORD

help → helpful

help is the base word inside helpful.

help · helper · helpful · helpless
🧱

ADD TO THE END

help + -ful = helpful

The suffix -ful can mean “full of” or “having.”

You are very helpful!
